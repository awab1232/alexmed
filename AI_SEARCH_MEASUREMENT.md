# AI Search Measurement — NiroLearn

This file defines measurement, not guarantees. Search visibility, AI answers, and citations depend on external systems.

## Google Search Console

Track weekly:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- CTR
- Average position
- Queries
- Pages
- Branded vs non-branded queries
- Arabic vs English queries
- Pages gaining/losing impressions
- Query/page cannibalization

Priority filters:

- `NiroLearn`
- Arabic brand variants
- AI study assistant queries
- PDF study queries
- Flashcard queries
- Exam preparation queries
- Protected question-set queries

## Bing Webmaster Tools / AI Performance

Where available, track:

- Search performance
- AI citations
- Cited URLs
- Grounding queries
- Topics
- Intents
- Citation share

Do not describe these as rankings or guaranteed AI placements.

## Review cadence

- Weekly for the first 60 days after publishing `/learn`.
- Monthly after stable indexing.
- Update the content roadmap based on pages with real impressions and useful engagement.

## External actions required

- Verify domain property in Google Search Console.
- Submit `https://nirolearn.com/sitemap.xml`.
- Configure Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Confirm official social/profile URLs before adding `sameAs`.
