# NiroLearn Content Quality Gate

Every new public article or guide must pass these gates before it becomes indexable.

## Search intent

- One clear primary intent.
- No duplicate page targeting the same query.
- No competitor pages unless there is a legitimate editorial reason.
- “Do not target” is acceptable for spammy or brand-confusing queries.

## Content quality

- Direct answer in the first 100–150 words.
- Clear H1 and descriptive H2s.
- Useful explanation, examples, and limitations.
- No keyword stuffing.
- No hidden text.
- No thin pages.
- No fabricated facts, statistics, testimonials, reviews, awards, or affiliations.

## Product accuracy

- Mention only features that exist or are explicitly marked as planned.
- Educational games pages must mention only real NiroLearn games.
- Protected question-set pages must not expose private implementation details.

## SEO basics

- Title and meta description are unique.
- Canonical URL is correct.
- Page is in sitemap only when valuable and indexable.
- Private routes remain excluded.
- Structured data matches visible content.
- Related articles and product links are natural.

## Accessibility and UX

- Works on mobile.
- Arabic pages are RTL.
- English text remains readable LTR where appropriate.
- Links have descriptive text.
- No unnecessary client-side JavaScript.

## Maintenance

- Show “Last updated” only when the date is real.
- Review core guides after product changes.
- Update or deindex pages that become outdated or low-value.
