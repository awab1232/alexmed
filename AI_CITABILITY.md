# AI Citability Plan — NiroLearn

## What helps discoverability

- The public pages are server-rendered and explain real product capabilities.
- Headings and answers are direct and readable.
- The visible FAQ content matches the JSON-LD FAQ markup.
- Canonical URLs are explicit.
- The schema should stay limited to facts the page actually supports.
- `public/llms.txt` can help some crawlers understand the site structure.

## Important limits

- `llms.txt` is non-standard.
- No mechanism guarantees inclusion in AI Overviews or model citations.
- Visibility in AI search systems is a measurement outcome, not a promise.

## Editorial rules

- Verify every claim before publishing it.
- Prefer primary and academic sources for learning-science claims.
- Do not fabricate citations.
- Do not mass-generate thin or duplicated pages.
- Keep new content tied to real product capabilities and real search intent.

## Practical direction

Use the current content architecture to earn citations through clarity, canonical URLs, and substantiated claims — not through invented authority signals.
